## 1. What TIRHON is, in one paragraph

TIRHON is a Kenya-based vehicle sourcing and import company. A customer tells us the vehicle they want and their budget. Our buying partner in Japan finds it at auction or from dealer stock, we show the customer the evidence before they pay, we handle the export, and we support them until the vehicle is cleared at Mombasa and in their hands. We do not sell cars from a yard. We deliver a **service**: the safe, transparent way to get exactly the car you want from Japan.

That sentence is the brand. Everything below serves it.

---

## 2. Purpose (why we exist)

> **To make importing a car from Japan something a Kenyan family or business can do with confidence.**

Kenyans lose money and sleep importing cars: wrong cars, hidden costs, silent sellers, disappearing deposits. TIRHON exists to remove that fear. Not by promising the cheapest car, but by making every step visible.

---

## 3. Mission (what we do every day)

> **We source the right vehicle from Japan for each customer, show the proof before payment, and guide them through every step to delivery in Kenya.**

Three commitments hidden in that sentence, and each one is measurable:
1. *The right vehicle* → the customer's model, year, budget and spec, not whatever we have.
2. *Proof before payment* → auction sheet, photos, inspection certificate, staged payments against documents.
3. *Every step to delivery* → milestone updates from auction to Mombasa gate.

---

## 4. Vision (where we are going)

> **To be the most trusted name in Kenya for importing vehicles from Japan.**

Not the biggest. Not the cheapest. The most trusted. That is a position no large exporter and no roadside yard currently owns, and it is the one that compounds through reviews and referrals.

Five-year picture: when a Kenyan says "I'm importing a car", a friend replies "use TIRHON, they show you everything."

---

## 5. Values (how we behave, with the behaviour each one demands)

Values only matter if the sales team can be judged against them on WhatsApp. Each value below has the behaviour it requires and the behaviour it forbids.

| Value | What it means in practice | What it forbids |
|---|---|---|
| **Transparency** | Every price is labelled (FOB, CFR Mombasa, estimated on-road). Every vehicle comes with its auction sheet and inspection report. Every delay is reported before the customer asks. | Unlabelled prices, "DM for price", hiding a lower auction grade, silence when a vessel is late. |
| **Trust is earned with proof** | We show documents, deliveries, the office and the team. Deposit only after the auction is won in the customer's name; balance only against the Bill of Lading. | "Trust me", asking for money before evidence, stock claims for cars we have not checked that week. |
| **Customer first, not car first** | We start from what the customer needs and their real budget, and we say honestly when a request does not make sense (for example a pre-2019 car). | Pushing a car because we have it, over-promising timelines, upselling beyond budget. |
| **Reliability** | We answer within minutes in business hours. We do what we said, on the date we said. | Unanswered messages, missed follow-ups, changing terms after agreement. |
| **Care beyond the sale** | The relationship ends when the customer is happy on the road, not when the deposit clears. Clearance guidance, delivery coordination, a check-in after handover. | Disappearing after the balance is paid. |

A useful internal test for any message, post or ad: **"Would we be comfortable if the customer showed this to a lawyer and to their mother?"**

## 7. Brand personality and voice

**If TIRHON were a person:** a calm, well-prepared Kenyan professional in their late 30s who has done this many times, explains things without jargon, never pressures, and always has the document ready before you ask. Confident because they have proof, not because they are loud.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Calm, specific, factual | Hype, shouting, "🔥🔥🔥" |
| Warm and plain-spoken | Corporate or stiff ("Dear Sir/Madam") |
| Premium in finish | Flashy or arrogant |
| Educational | Preachy |
| Kenyan and international at once | A Japan company with a Kenya phone number, or a Nairobi yard pretending to be in Japan |

**Voice rules for every channel:**
- Short sentences. Simple English. Swahili phrases are welcome in social captions where natural, never in legal or price text.
- Label every number. Date every price.
- One emoji maximum per WhatsApp message. None in ads or on the website.
- We say "we checked" and "here is the document", not "trust us".
- We never name-shame competitors. We compare structurally.

**Key messages to repeat everywhere:**
1. Tell us the model, year and budget. We check current Japan options for you.
2. Deposit only after the auction is won in your name. Balance only after the Bill of Lading.
3. Auction sheet and inspection certificate before you pay.
4. A team in Kenya you can meet, a buying partner in Japan who is at the auction.
5. A written estimate of the real cost to the road, not just the Japan price.

## 8. Brand architecture (how the companies appear to the customer)

- **TIRHON** is the only brand the customer sees. Website, WhatsApp, social, invoices, signage.
- **The Japan buying partner** is described as exactly that, by role, and shown on camera as proof. The partner's company name appears only in contracts and when a customer asks.
- **Markato** never appears to customers. It is the marketing and investment partner.
- **BestCars254** is not used going forward for TIRHON customers. Past deliveries under that name may be shown as "vehicles delivered to Mombasa through our Japan buying partner", with customer permission, and only if that is accurate.

## 9. Logo design brief (for the designer)

### 9.1 The one-sentence brief
Design a wordmark-led identity for **TIRHON**, a Kenyan vehicle-sourcing company that imports cars from Japan, that reads as **trustworthy, precise and premium**, and that works first on a WhatsApp profile circle and a phone screen.

### 9.2 Context the designer needs
- Customers are Kenyan adults, 28–55, mostly on Android phones, deciding whether to send a large sum of money to a company they found online. The logo's job is to look like a real, established company at 40 pixels wide.
- The market is full of red-and-black "car deals" logos with chrome, swooshes, speed lines and silhouettes of a Land Cruiser. We want the opposite of that noise.
- The name "TIRHON" is short, uncommon and has no existing associations. That is an asset: it can own a look.

### 9.3 Concept directions (designer explores two or three, we pick one)
1. **The Clear Wordmark.** TIRHON in a confident geometric or humanist sans-serif, custom-spaced, with one subtle detail (for example a bar or a notch in the O suggesting a route or a road) and no icon. Rationale: the most "established company" look; works everywhere.
2. **The Bridge / Route mark.** A minimal symbol suggesting a connection between two points (Japan → Kenya, or a shipping route) that also reads as a "T". Paired with the wordmark. Rationale: expresses the actual service, sourcing and bringing home, without drawing a car.
3. **The Seal.** A compact badge or monogram (T, or TA) that reads as a stamp of verification, echoing the "proof and documents" promise. Rationale: works well as a WhatsApp avatar and a watermark on vehicle photos and documents.

**Do not draw:** a car silhouette, a wheel, a steering wheel, a speedometer, a Japanese flag or rising sun, a Kenyan flag, chrome, gradients that imitate metal, italic "speed" type, swooshes.

### 9.4 Colour direction (recommendation, designer refines)
- **Primary:** a deep, serious blue or blue-black (navy / ink). Trust, stability, documents.
- **Accent:** one warm colour used sparingly for CTAs and highlights. Amber/gold (premium, "sunrise", also reads well against navy) or a muted terracotta (Kenyan earth). The designer chooses one, not both.
- **Neutrals:** off-white and warm greys for backgrounds, so vehicle photos look clean.
- **Avoid:** red as a primary (dominant in this category; verify current competitor palettes before finalising), bright green, neon anything.
- Must pass contrast checks for text on WhatsApp dark mode and light mode.

### 9.5 Typography
- Wordmark: geometric or humanist sans-serif, wide letter-spacing, capitals. Examples of the feel: Inter, Manrope, DM Sans, Söhne-like. Free or open-licensed fonts preferred so the sales team can use the same font in Canva and WhatsApp graphics.
- Body font for all materials: the same family or a close free equivalent, so nothing needs a licence.

### 9.6 Must work at these sizes and places (test each)
- WhatsApp Business profile picture (circle, ~40 px on screen)
- Instagram / TikTok avatar (circle)
- Facebook page cover and Meta ad end-card
- Watermark on vehicle photos (single-colour, semi-transparent)
- Header of a proforma invoice and the landed-cost estimate PDF
- Office signage and a car-window sticker at handover
- Favicon (16 px)

### 9.7 Deliverables from the designer
- Primary logo (horizontal), stacked version, icon/monogram alone, single-colour black, single-colour white.
- Colour codes (HEX, RGB, CMYK), font names and licence links.
- Files: SVG, PNG (transparent, 512, 1024, 2048 px), PDF; plus a Canva-ready PNG set.
- A one-page usage sheet: clear space, minimum size, do-not-do examples.
- Social templates (optional but valuable): vehicle card, "options this week" carousel frame, document-proof frame, testimonial frame, WhatsApp Status template.

### 9.8 Timeline and approval
- Day 0: Kenya side confirms Section 0. Brief sent to designer.
- Day 5: two or three concept directions, each shown as a WhatsApp avatar, a vehicle photo watermark and an invoice header, not just on a white canvas.
- Day 7: Kenya side and Markato choose one direction. Approval criteria: does it look like a company you would send money to, and is it legible at 40 px? Aesthetics come third.
- Day 10: final files. Rollout the same day on WhatsApp, Meta, Google Business Profile, invoice templates.
