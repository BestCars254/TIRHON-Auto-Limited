## Executive summary (read this if nothing else)

TIRHON has a real structural advantage that almost no Kenyan Facebook car seller has: a Japan-side buying partner with auction access, and a Kenya-side company that can meet the customer face to face. The marketing job is to make that advantage visible, provable and easy to act on.

The business is a sourcing service, not a stockist. So the offer is not "cars for sale". The offer is: **tell us the model, year and budget; we check live Japan options and guide you to Mombasa.** That is what every ad, page and WhatsApp reply must sell.

The biggest opportunity in the next 90 days is not a creative idea. It is **closing the loop between ad spend and deposits**. Until every WhatsApp lead is logged with its source, campaign and outcome, we are spending blind.

**The five things we must execute first** (full detail in the final section):

1. Tracking and a lead register before any paid spend.
2. One clear offer and one landing page with a WhatsApp CTA.
3. A WhatsApp qualification script with a 5-minute response target.
4. A Meta WhatsApp-conversation campaign in Nairobi only, three creatives, two weeks.
5. A weekly sales-to-marketing report (leads, qualified, quotes, deposits, lost reasons).

## 1. Assessment of current positioning

**ASSUMPTION:** TIRHON is at or near launch. There is no live website in this repository, no verified ad account history, and no CRM. I am treating the marketing function as starting from zero. If a page, a Facebook page or an ad account already exists, send me the links and I will re-audit.

**What exists on the Japan side (from the BestCars254 sales playbook, VERIFIED internal):**

- A working sales process: proforma → 30% deposit after the auction is won → auction sheet and photos → balance before shipping against the Bill of Lading → roughly 45 to 50 days from deposit to driving.
- Known objections, in order: price, delivery time, trust, payment terms.
- Buyers shop three or more exporters at once. Response speed and photo quality win.
- Registered Japan company (Ligarefy) and a Kenya contact.

**Positioning today:** undefined. Right now TIRHON is indistinguishable from the hundreds of "Japan cars, DM for price" pages on Facebook. That is the problem to fix, and it is fixable because the underlying proof exists (real shipments, real documents, a real Kenya office).

**What the market has changed in 2026 (VERIFIED, source A1–A3):**

- Since 1 January 2026 KEBS enforces the 8-year rule so that only right-hand-drive vehicles **first registered 2019 or later** clear Mombasa. Vehicles registered 2018 or earlier arriving after 31 December 2025 are rejected at the importer's expense.
- Passenger-car import duty is 35%. Excise duty is 20% to 35% by engine size and fuel type. VAT 16%, Import Declaration Fee 2.5%, Railway Development Levy 2%. Duties are calculated on the KRA Current Retail Selling Price (CRSP) schedule with age depreciation, not on our invoice.
- A KEBS-approved pre-shipment Certificate of Roadworthiness is mandatory. Without it the car does not clear and attracts a penalty.

**Why this matters for marketing:**

- The 2019 cut-off pushes the entry price up. Cheap 2015–2018 Prados are gone from the import market. This favours TIRHON's stated focus on higher-value vehicles, and it means the "compare Japan to local" argument must be made on **2019+ vehicles** only.
- **A real, honest urgency exists for Q4 2026:** any 2019-registered vehicle must land in Mombasa by 31 December 2026 or it becomes ineligible (ASSUMPTION based on the 2018/2025 pattern; confirm with KEBS before use in ads). With ~45 days transit, that means auction purchase by roughly late October. This is a legitimate campaign angle for October. It is also a compliance risk: we must not sell a 2019 car in November.
- Landed-cost confusion is the single biggest cause of lost deals in this category. Marketing must own the "what will it actually cost me on the road" question.

## 3. Our strongest value propositions

In order of strength, each with the proof we need to make it believable.

1. **"You choose the car. We source it live from Japan."** Custom sourcing beats browsing someone else's stock. Proof: screen recordings of auction searches for a real customer request (blur personal data).
2. **"A company in Kenya you can meet, backed by a buying partner in Japan."** This is the trust bridge. Most exporters are either only in Japan or only a yard in Nairobi. Proof: office video, team on camera, Japan-side partner on camera.
3. **"Pay in stages, against documents."** Deposit only after the auction is won in your name, balance only against the Bill of Lading. Proof: a one-page "How your money is protected" explainer and the actual document types shown on screen.
4. **"Know the real cost before you commit."** A written landed-cost estimate including KRA duty on the CRSP basis, port charges and clearing. Proof: a worked example on the landing page, updated when KRA changes rates.
5. **"Inspection report and auction sheet before you pay."** Proof: annotated auction sheet explainer content.
6. **"We stay with you through Mombasa."** Clearance guidance and delivery coordination. Proof: port arrival photos, customer handover photos.

**Weakest claim, do not lead with it:** "cheaper than local". Sometimes true, often not once duty is included on a 2019+ vehicle. Lead with control, transparency and safety; let the price comparison be a calculator, not a slogan.

## 4. Our biggest marketing weaknesses

Ranked by how much revenue they cost us.

1. **No closed-loop tracking.** No way to say which ad produced a deposit. This blocks every optimisation decision. Fix first.
2. **No proof library.** Testimonials, delivery photos, documents and customer intros exist in WhatsApp threads, not in a usable, permissioned asset bank.
3. **Undefined offer.** "Import from Japan" is a category, not an offer. We need one sentence the sales team, the ads and the page all repeat.
4. **Response time risk.** Buyers message three exporters at once. If a lead waits two hours, we have lost the comparison. (ASSUMPTION: there is no rota or SLA yet.)
5. **Landed-cost ambiguity.** If we cannot give a credible on-the-road estimate within one message, the conversation dies on "let me think".
6. **No brand assets.** No logo system, no photo style, no template for vehicle cards. This makes every post look like every competitor.
7. **Single-channel dependency.** All demand is planned through Meta. Google Search intent ("import Prado Kenya") is where the highest-intent buyer sits and we are not there.
8. **Two-company confusion.** If Ligarefy, BestCars254 and TIRHON all appear to a customer, trust drops. The customer must see one brand: TIRHON. Ligarefy appears only as "our Japan buying partner" when asked.

## 5. Competitors we should study

Three groups. Study each for offer, pricing display, trust signals, CTA, and gaps.

**Group A — Japan-side exporters selling direct to Kenyans (VERIFIED they operate in the market, A4):**
BE FORWARD, SBT Japan, Car From Japan, AA Japan, Autocom Japan, PicknBuy24, TokyoCarZ.

- Strength: huge stock, CIF/CFR prices on every listing, decades of SEO, Kenya-specific landing pages.
- Weakness: no one to meet in Kenya, generic support, customers hear horror stories about condition mismatches, and they cannot do custom auction sourcing at a personal level.
- Gap for us: **human, local, custom.**

**Group B — Kenya-based import agents / auction brokers (VERIFIED they exist, A4):**
Car Imports Kenya (carimports.co.ke), Car Imports 254, CarsKenya, Maridady Motors, Khushi Motors, Auto Kenya, Kai & Karo.

- These are our direct competitors. Same model as ours. Study their WhatsApp flow by becoming a mystery shopper: send the same Prado request to five of them and log response time, what they ask, how they quote, and what proof they send. Do this in week 1.
- Gap to find: most will quote vaguely or slowly, or push stock they already hold.

**Group C — Local yards and marketplaces:**
Nairobi showrooms on Ngong Road, Kiambu Road and Mombasa Road; Jiji; Facebook Marketplace.

- They win on "drive it today". We do not compete on speed. We compete on spec choice, condition transparency and price for a like-for-like 2019+ vehicle.

**Do not copy** any of them. Identify the gap: nobody is clearly the **transparent, meet-us-in-Nairobi, custom-sourcing** brand. That is the slot.

## 7. Recommended offer

**Primary offer (the only one for the first 60 days):**

> **Free Japan Vehicle Search.** Tell us the model, year and your maximum budget. Within 24 hours we send you current Japan options with photos, grade and an estimated cost to Mombasa. No obligation.

Why this offer:
- Low friction: it asks for information, not money.
- Self-qualifying: budget and year are the two fields that reveal whether the lead is real.
- Demonstrates the service: the response *is* the product.
- Creates a reason to follow up: "your options" is a natural second message.

**Operational requirement:** the 24-hour promise must be true. If the Japan side cannot turn searches around within a business day, the offer says "within 48 hours". Do not promise what we cannot do.

**Secondary offers (introduce after week 4, one at a time, test):**
- **Landed-Cost Estimate:** "Send us the car listing you are looking at, anywhere online. We will tell you what it will really cost on the road in Kenya." Captures people already shopping BE FORWARD or SBT.
- **Model Buyer's Guide download** (Prado, Harrier, CX-5): email capture for the nurture list.
- **Q4 2019-eligibility campaign** (October only, once verified with KEBS): "2019 vehicles must land before 31 December. Here is the last safe order date."

**Not recommended:** discounts, "free shipping", "deposit refund guarantees" we have not legally structured.

## 10. WhatsApp funnel

**Goal:** first human reply within 5 minutes during business hours, under 60 minutes outside them. Track it.

**Step 1 — Auto-reply (instant, WhatsApp Business greeting):**

> Thank you for contacting TIRHON Auto Limited.
> To find the right vehicle from Japan for you, please send:
> 1. Preferred model
> 2. Preferred year (2019 or newer for Kenya import)
> 3. Maximum budget (KES)
> 4. Your location in Kenya
> 5. When you plan to buy
> A team member will reply shortly.

**Step 2 — Human qualification (within 5 min):** confirm the five answers, add two questions naturally: "Is this cash or financing?" and "Have you imported before?" Classify:

| Class | Rule | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Hot** | Realistic budget for the model and year, buying within 30 days, cash or approved financing | Japan search within 24h. Salesperson owns. Follow up daily. |
| **Warm** | Realistic budget, 1–3 months, or needs education | Send buyer's guide + one example option. Follow-up cadence: day 2, 5, 10, 20. |
| **Cold** | 3+ months, or vague budget | Add to nurture (WhatsApp broadcast list + email). Monthly touch. |
| **Unqualified** | Budget below floor for the model, pre-2019 request, or outside our service | Polite decline with one useful pointer. Log the reason. |

**Step 3 — Options message (Hot/Warm):** 2 to 4 vehicles, each with: year, grade, mileage, colour, 3 photos, CFR Mombasa estimate, on-road estimate range, "auction date". Then one question: "Which of these is closest to what you want?"

**Step 4 — Quote follow-up:** day 1 ("Any questions on the options?"), day 3 (send proof: a delivery photo or testimonial, no pressure), day 7 (auction prices move: "Still interested? I can re-check this week's listings"), day 14 (close politely, move to nurture).

**Step 5 — Deposit follow-up:** after proforma, confirm receipt within 24h; if silent at 48h, call.

**Step 6 — Shipment milestones (post-deposit, automatic):** auction won → inspection booked → inspection passed → vessel booked → B/L issued → vessel departed → ETA Mombasa → arrived → cleared → delivered. Each milestone is one message with a photo or document. This is also our best future content.

**Step 7 — Post-delivery:** day 3 check-in, day 14 review + referral ask, day 90 "how is the car?" and service tip.

**Tooling:** WhatsApp Business app now; upgrade to the WhatsApp Business Platform (API) via a provider once volume exceeds what two people can handle. Every conversation gets a lead ID in the CRM the same day.

## 12. Meta Ads strategy

**Phase 1 (weeks 1–4): validate the offer in Nairobi.**

- **Objective:** Engagement → Messaging destinations → WhatsApp (click-to-WhatsApp).
- **Geo:** Nairobi + 40 km. One ad set. Do not split by city yet; the algorithm needs volume.
- **Audience:** Broad, 28–50, all genders (do not exclude women; family SUV decisions are joint). Optional second ad set: interests SUV, Toyota Land Cruiser, car import. Compare.
- **Creatives (3 at launch, section 18):** talking-head "how the search works", document-proof "what you see before you pay", vehicle carousel "2019+ options we checked this week".
- **Budget:** ESTIMATE KES 3,000–5,000 per day for the ad set. Enough for 50+ conversations in two weeks.
- **KPI:** cost per *qualified* conversation. Raw cost per conversation is a diagnostic, not a goal.
- **Stop condition:** after 14 days, if fewer than 25% of conversations are Hot or Warm, pause and fix the offer or the auto-reply before spending more.
- **Scale condition:** cost per qualified conversation stable for 7 days and sales confirm quality → raise budget 20% every 3 days, add Mombasa and Nakuru as separate ad sets.

**Phase 2 (weeks 5–12):**
- Add a Lead Form campaign (Instant Form) for people who will not open WhatsApp from an ad; compare quality.
- Add retargeting (section 14).
- Build a Lookalike from Hot leads once we have 100+.
- Test Advantage+ placements vs manual Feed + Reels.

**Testing discipline:** one variable per test, 7-day windows, minimum 30 conversations per cell before judging. Keep a test log (hypothesis, variable, result, decision).

**Meta metrics we watch daily:** CPM, CTR (link), cost per conversation, conversations started rate. Weekly: qualified rate, quote rate, deposit rate, cost per deposit.

## 19. Email marketing strategy

Email is secondary to WhatsApp in Kenya but valuable for Warm and Cold leads, diaspora buyers, and anyone who came through the form or a guide download.

**Tool:** Brevo or MailerLite (free tiers, WhatsApp-adjacent features). Segment by model interest and classification.

**New-lead sequence (6 emails over 14 days):**

3. Day 4: *How we verify condition before you pay* (auction sheet, inspection certificate).
4. Day 7: *Popular 2019+ SUVs for Kenya and what they typically cost on the road* (dated ESTIMATES).
5. Day 10: *A customer's story* (video).
6. Day 14: *Ready for your personalised search?* One CTA to WhatsApp.

**Re-engagement (monthly to Cold):** market update, KRA or KEBS changes, one testimonial, one CTA.

## 20. Lead-nurture strategy

Different rhythm per classification, all logged in the CRM.

- **Hot:** human, daily until options sent, then day 1/3/7/14 cadence. Phone call at day 3 if silent.
- **Warm:** options + guide, then WhatsApp touches day 2/5/10/20, then monthly. Move to Hot on any buying signal (asks for proforma, asks about payment, gives a date).
- **Cold:** monthly WhatsApp broadcast (opt-in) + email. Retargeting audience.
- **Lost — price:** re-contact at 60 days with "auction prices moved" if true.
- **Lost — trust:** add to the retargeting trust sequence; re-contact at 30 days with a new customer story.
- **Lost — went local:** re-contact at 6 months ("next upgrade?").
- **Customers:** post-delivery day 3/14/90, then quarterly. Referral ask at day 14 with a simple, honest incentive (e.g. a contribution toward their next service; decide amount with finance).

The sales team reports every lost reason. Marketing turns the top three lost reasons each month into content.

## 22. Tracking requirements

Non-negotiable before paid spend starts.

1. **Meta Business Suite:** Pixel on the landing page; Conversions API via the site platform; WhatsApp Business number connected to the Facebook Page so click-to-WhatsApp conversations are attributed.
2. **Campaign codes in pre-filled WhatsApp messages:** every ad, post, Status and email link opens WhatsApp with a code (e.g. `[META-PRADO-01]`). The sales agent copies the code into the CRM. Low-tech and it works.

5. **CRM fields** for source/campaign/ad on every lead (section 11).

7. **Response-time logging:** first-reply timestamp on every lead.
8. **Consent:** the auto-reply states we may contact them about vehicle options; customer-list uploads to Meta use only leads who did not object.
